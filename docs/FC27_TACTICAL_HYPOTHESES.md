# FC 27 Tactical Hypotheses Registry

> **Global Classification Defaults**:
> - `SOURCE_CLASSIFICATION=EXTERNAL_TACTICAL_HYPOTHESIS`
> - `EAST_COASTERS_VALIDATION=NOT_RUN`
> - `CURRENT_CONFIDENCE=UNVERIFIED`

---

## 1. Governance Policy for Tactical Claims

External community, creator, and competitive meta claims (spanning EA SPORTS FC 25, FC 26, and early FC 27 Pro Clubs theorycrafting) **must never** be presented as verified East Coasters (`club_id=330323`, `common-gen5`) facts or hard-coded into `src/fc27_analytics.py`.

Under `src/fc27_coaching.py` (`validate_coaching_recommendation`), `src/fc27_search_scout.py` (`FC27Hypothesis`), and `src/fc27_analyst.py` (`FC27AnalystFinding`, `FC27AnalystReport`):
1. Every external tactical claim is registered in this document as an `EXTERNAL_TACTICAL_HYPOTHESIS` with `approval_eligibility: NOT_APPROVABLE` and is never auto-promoted by Search, Scout, or Analyst.
2. In contrast, internal observational hypotheses and findings generated by `build_fc27_scout_report` (`src/fc27_search_scout.py`) and `build_fc27_analyst_report` (`src/fc27_analyst.py`) are derived strictly from normalized East Coasters match evidence (`source_classification="EAST_COASTERS_NORMALIZED_EVIDENCE"`, `status="OBSERVATIONAL_ONLY"`, `recommendation_status="NOT_A_RECOMMENDATION"`, `approvable=False`, `dispatchable=False`, `FORMULA_VERSION="fc27-v1.0"`), cap confidence at the underlying evidence tier (`INSUFFICIENT_DATA`, `EXPLORATORY`, `LOW_CONFIDENCE`, `MODERATE_CONFIDENCE`, `HIGH_CONFIDENCE`), and use correlation-only phrasing with explicit `counter_evidence`, `limitations`, `confounders`, `competing_explanations`, `falsification_criteria`, and `additional_data_needed`.
3. No tactical hypothesis may enter a Commissioner-approvable coaching package until East Coasters has accumulated the required minimum sample size (`minimum_data_required`) of verified `COMPLETED` matches in PostgreSQL 16 and passed controlled pre/post comparison without failing its `rejection_criteria`.
